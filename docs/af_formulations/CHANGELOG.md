## v0.1 · First release, for review · 2026-11-04 (latest)

Code and page source: [`v0.1`](https://github.com/CC4A-SSA/cc4adapt/tree/v0.1) · [page](./)

First version shared with colleagues for comments. Exploratory: every number is illustrative.

- Part 1: how six rules for splitting a practice's benefit into production and adaptation change the result across maize districts in Kenya, Ethiopia and Nigeria, using Atlas hazard frequencies (generic drought, heat and waterlogging hazards on the maize growing season, 1995–2014) and MapSPAM yields; includes a stress-benchmark sensitivity.
- Part 2: ERA maize trials for cover crops and reduced tillage, each season classed as stress or normal from observed weather, giving the practices' effects in each; a check of Atlas stress frequencies against observed weather and climate models in three countries.
- Methods, assumptions and sources of error, with a publication-style methods document linked to the code.
- A Comment button on each section (GitHub sign-in), light and dark themes, and this version history.
